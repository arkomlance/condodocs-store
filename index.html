Florida · Meetings

Florida board meeting notices: 48 hours, 7 days, or 14?

Post the notice 48 hours ahead, or mail it 7 days out — and stretch that to 14 days when the meeting takes up special assessments or changes to parcel-use rules. Annual member meetings need 14 days' notice. The packet's cheat sheet puts every deadline on one page so the board stops guessing.

Cited in the packet: Fla. Stat. §720.303 (board meetings) · §720.306 (member meetings)
New York · Foundations

There is no "New York HOA Act" — and that's the guide

New York has no single HOA statute. Incorporated HOAs run on the Not-for-Profit Corporation Law plus their own declaration and bylaws; condos sit under Real Property Law Article 9-B. Every NY guide in the packet says this up front, then works from the N-PCL — annual meetings, special meetings on 10% member demand, records inspection, and the fiduciary standard.

Cited in the packet: N-PCL §603 (meetings) · §621 (records) · §717 (director duties)
Florida · Enforcement

Fines & hearings: the $100 / $1,000 rule and the 14-day letter

A Florida fine runs $100 per violation, capped at $1,000 in aggregate unless your documents say otherwise — and no fine stands without at least 14 days' written notice and a hearing before a committee of three or more members who aren't officers, directors, or their household. The packet's letter ladder is built around exactly that sequence.

Cited in the packet: Fla. Stat. §720.305 (fines & hearings) · §720.3085 (liens)
